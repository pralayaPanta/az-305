### Question 9

You need to ensure that you can restore the last uploaded version of `File1.txt` from any day for up to 30 days after the file was overwritten. The solution must minimize storage space.

What should you include in the solution?

A) Container soft delete with a 30-day retention period  
B) Automated daily blob snapshots of `File1.txt` retained for 30 days  
C) Blob soft delete with a 30-day retention period  
D) Blob versioning across the entire container  

> [!faq]- Show Answer & Explanation
> **Answer: B** — If you enabled blob versioning, a new version would be generated **every hour** (24 versions per day × 30 days = 720 versions), consuming massive storage space.
